06 — QUICK ANSWERS

GOOD TO
KNOW.

A few important things to remember when dealing with account restrictions.

Can this website unban my account? +

No. This is an informational community page. Only official game support can review account-specific cases.

Can anyone guarantee an unban? +

No third party can honestly guarantee a result. Be cautious of people asking for money, passwords or verification codes.

What should I include in an appeal? +

Follow the official support form's instructions, explain the issue clearly and provide only accurate information requested by the official channel.

Is it safe to share my login details? +

No. Never share your password, one-time code, recovery code or other secret login information with anyone.
